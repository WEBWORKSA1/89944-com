About 89944 Price Lab

Free tools and plain-English guides that make the psychology and math of prices clear.

Our mission

Prices are the most powerful — and most neglected — number in any business. A single percentage point of price improvement typically has a larger effect on profit than the same improvement in volume or costs. Yet most founders set prices once, copy a competitor and never revisit them. Shoppers, meanwhile, face package sizes and promotions engineered to blur comparison.

89944 Price Lab exists to make pricing clear. We build free, private, instant calculators and write plain-English guides grounded in established marketing and finance principles.

Why "8 · 99 · 44"?

The domain's digits read like a price tag: an 8, a 99 ending and a 44. The 99 ending is the most common in retail. We use the number as a memorable name, not a claim to any product, slogan or brand.

Editorial standards

  • Every formula is shown so you can check our math.
  • Examples are illustrative and labelled as such.
  • Rates that change frequently (such as AI model prices) are user-editable rather than hard-coded.
  • Sponsored content is always clearly labelled and never changes a tool's output.
  • We correct errors promptly — tell us if you find one.

How we're funded

Advertising (including Google AdSense), clearly-labelled sponsorships, optional paid pricing services and voluntary support from users.
