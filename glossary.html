📖 Pricing glossary

The language of pricing in plain English.

AnchoringBreak-even pointCACCharm pricingContribution marginCPIDecoy effectDynamic pricingElasticityFreemiumGross marginLeft-digit effectLTVMarkupPenetration pricingPrestige pricingPrice skimmingShrinkflationToken (AI)Unit priceValue-based pricingVan Westendorp

Anchoring

Using a reference price (e.g., a crossed-out original or premium tier) to make another price feel reasonable.

Break-even point

The sales volume at which total revenue equals total costs.

CAC

Customer acquisition cost — total sales & marketing spend divided by new customers.

Charm pricing

Setting prices just below a round number, such as $9.99.

Contribution margin

Price minus variable cost per unit.

CPI

Consumer Price Index — a measure of the average change in prices paid by consumers.

Decoy effect

Adding a less attractive option to steer buyers toward a target option.

Dynamic pricing

Adjusting prices in real time based on demand, inventory or competitors.

Elasticity

How strongly demand responds to a price change.

Freemium

A free tier with paid upgrades.

Gross margin

(Revenue − cost of goods sold) ÷ revenue.

Left-digit effect

The tendency to judge a number mainly by its first digit.

LTV

Customer lifetime value — gross profit expected from a customer over their lifetime.

Markup

(Price − cost) ÷ cost.

Penetration pricing

Launching at a low price to win share quickly.

Prestige pricing

Using round, higher prices to signal quality.

Price skimming

Launching high and lowering the price over time.

Shrinkflation

Reducing product size while keeping the price the same.

Token (AI)

A chunk of text, roughly ¾ of an English word, used to bill LLM usage.

Unit price

Price per standard unit (100 g, litre, item) for fair comparison.

Value-based pricing

Setting price according to the value delivered to the customer rather than cost.

Van Westendorp

A survey method that finds an acceptable price range by asking four price questions.
