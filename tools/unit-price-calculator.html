Why unit price beats sticker price

Packaging sizes are designed to make comparison hard. "Family size" is not always cheaper per gram, and promotional multi-buys can be worse value than the regular large pack. Unit price — the cost per 100 g, per litre or per item — is the only fair comparison.

Watch out for shrinkflation

When a pack shrinks but the price stays the same, the sticker price hides a price increase. Re-check unit prices on products you buy often, and compare against the store brand.

Tips

  • Bulk only wins if you'll use it before it expires.
  • Compare like with like: concentrated detergents need "per load", not per ml.
  • Shelf-label unit prices sometimes use different units across products — this tool normalises them.

FAQ

Can I compare ounces with grams?

Yes. The tool converts weight units (g, kg, oz, lb) and volume units (ml, L, fl oz) to a common base.

Is bigger always cheaper?

No — sale prices on small packs often beat regular prices on big packs. That's exactly what this tool catches.
