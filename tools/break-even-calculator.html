The break-even formula

Break-even units = Fixed costs ÷ (Price − Variable cost per unit)

The denominator is your contribution margin: what each sale contributes toward fixed costs after paying for itself. Once fixed costs are covered, every additional unit's contribution margin is profit.

Three levers to lower break-even

  1. Raise price — usually the fastest lever, because it flows 100% to contribution margin.
  2. Cut variable cost — negotiate suppliers, reduce packaging, lower payment fees.
  3. Cut fixed cost — the slowest lever, but it lowers risk permanently.

Use it for launches and campaigns

Treat a marketing campaign's spend as a fixed cost and divide by contribution margin to find how many sales the campaign must drive to pay for itself. That number is your minimum acceptable result before you launch.

FAQ

What counts as a fixed cost?

Costs that don't change with volume in the short term: rent, base salaries, subscriptions, insurance, loan payments.

Is break-even the same as payback period?

No. Break-even is about volume per period; payback is how long it takes to recover an upfront investment.
