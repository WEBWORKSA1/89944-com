Why freelancers undercharge

Most people divide their old salary by 2,080 hours. But freelancers pay their own taxes, benefits and tools, take unpaid time off, and spend a third or more of their time on sales and admin. The result: a rate that looks fine but produces a salary far below target.

Rate = (Income + Expenses) ÷ (1 − Tax rate) ÷ (Hours/week × Working weeks × Billable %)

Then price above the minimum

This calculator gives you a floor. Move toward value-based pricing — per project, per outcome or on retainer — as soon as you can estimate the value you deliver. A website that brings a client $200,000 a year is not worth "40 hours × rate".

FAQ

Should I show my hourly rate?

Many consultants quote per project instead, so clients judge the outcome rather than the hours.

How often should I raise my rate?

Review at least yearly, and for every new client once your calendar is consistently full.
